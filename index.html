    About

    I am a quantitative education researcher. I build cognitive diagnostic assessments from the research-based assessments introductory physics courses already use, and I study equity in student outcomes with cross-institutional data from LASSO.

    Assessments like the Force Concept Inventory usually report one total score after a course ends. Using evidence-centered design and cognitive diagnostic models, I turn their items into tools that show which learning objectives each student has mastered during the semester. This work underlies the Mechanics Cognitive Diagnostic.

    Guided by QuantCrit, I use intersectional methods such as hierarchical models with LASSO data to locate differences in outcomes across student groups. I frame these differences as educational debts society owes to students (Ladson-Billings, 2006).
