    About Publications Presentations Research Teaching Service

    I study how introductory physics assessments can tell us what each student has mastered, and who our courses are leaving behind.

    My work combines cognitive diagnostic modeling, Bayesian hierarchical models, and intersectional quantitative methods to move past total scores toward fine-grained learning objectives. Much of it uses data from LASSO, a national platform that hosts research-based assessments for college STEM courses.

    Before coming to Iowa State, I earned a B.S. in Physics in Ho Chi Minh City, taught high school physics in Vietnam, completed an M.S. in Education at the University of Dayton, and taught general and university physics at Tennessee Wesleyan University.
